Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Velora is a speech practice app designed to provide a calm, supportive experience for people who stutter. Your privacy is important to us, and Velora is designed to work offline without accounts, advertising, or user tracking.

1. Information We Process

Velora uses your microphone and Apple's on-device speech recognition to support interactive speaking sessions. Speech audio and recognized words are processed locally on your device.

Velora does not upload your voice recordings, speech transcripts, or practice results to external servers.

2. Audio Recordings

During Rhythm Pacing, Velora temporarily records audio so you can listen to your performance on the Feedback screen.

These recordings are stored locally and are deleted when the session ends or the recording is no longer needed. If a session is interrupted unexpectedly, leftover recordings are cleaned up when recording storage is prepared for a future session.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Velora uses local device storage to remember simple preferences, such as whether you have completed onboarding or viewed technique introductions.

Practice results are maintained locally during app use. Velora does not create an online account or upload this information.

4. Permissions

Velora requests access to your microphone and speech recognition only when needed for speaking features.

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

5. Data Sharing and Tracking

Velora does not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies. We do not sell or share your speech data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Velora's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.
