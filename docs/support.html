Microphone or speech recognition isn't working

Velora needs microphone and speech recognition permission for interactive speaking features. Open your iPhone or iPad Settings, locate Velora in the Apps section, and check its available permissions. You can also review microphone and speech recognition permissions under Privacy & Security.

Can I use Velora offline?

Velora is designed for offline practice using on-device speech processing. Speech recognition availability can depend on your device's supported language settings.

Where can I replay my practice?

Audio playback is available in the Feedback phase for the previous Rhythm Pacing performance. Recordings are temporary and stored locally on your device.
